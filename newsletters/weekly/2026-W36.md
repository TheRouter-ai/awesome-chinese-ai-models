## New Models & Releases

**Tencent Hunyuan Hy4-preview** — Tencent published **[Hy4-preview](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-preview)** and **[Hy4-preview-FP8](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-preview-FP8)** on Hugging Face, with the official **[Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy4-preview](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy4-preview)** repository tracked the same week. The model-card metadata tags Hy4 as a Hunyuan MoE conversational text-generation model, and the FP8 checkpoint signals immediate attention to lower-cost serving or evaluation paths.

**Tencent ContextPilot** — Tencent added three ContextPilot checkpoints: **[ContextPilot-8B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/ContextPilot-8B)**, **[ContextPilot-14B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/ContextPilot-14B)**, and **[ContextPilot-E4B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/ContextPilot-E4B)**. The E4B variant also carries image-text-to-text metadata, suggesting Tencent is experimenting with compact agent models across both text and multimodal surfaces.

**Qwen3.8-Flash-Next** — Qwen published or updated **[Qwen3.8-Flash-Next](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next)** and **[Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8)**, with the official **[QwenLM/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next)** repository describing it as a foundation model from Alibaba's Qwen Team. The Hugging Face metadata tags it for image-text-to-text and conversational use, and includes eval-results and endpoint-compatible signals.

**Tencent WeMM Embedding** — Tencent added **[WeMM-Embedding-2B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/WeMM-Embedding-2B)**, **[WeMM-Embedding-4B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/WeMM-Embedding-4B)**, and **[WeMM-Embedding-9B](https://huggingface.co/tencent/WeMM-Embedding-9B)**. The metadata points to multimodal embeddings across text and image retrieval, built on Qwen-family vision-language backbones and sentence-transformers style usage.
